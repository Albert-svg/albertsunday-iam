About

I'm Albert, an IAM Engineer at Excellenta Cyber, based in Mauritius. I help the firm's clients design, implement and tune identity platforms, from first Okta tenant to mature, governed access across the Microsoft security stack.

I bring a background that runs from network engineering and database operations through to senior systems administration. That breadth means I think about identity as part of the whole environment, not a login screen bolted on top.

I hold an MSc in Business Information Systems Management from Middlesex University, which is a fair summary of how I work: technical depth, pointed at business outcomes.
